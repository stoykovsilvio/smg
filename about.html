Our story

Built on care,
not corners cut.

A small, trusted team working across Gloucestershire — bringing the same careful hands and high standards to every home and premises we touch.

Every job we take on gets the same level of care — the kind you'd want in your own home.

SMG Property Maintenance was founded with a simple idea: that painting, decorating and property maintenance should be done properly, first time, every time. No cutting corners. No leaving a mess. No vague timelines.

Today we work with homeowners, landlords, letting agents and businesses right across Gloucestershire — handling everything from a single freshly painted room to a full commercial repaint. The same care, the same standards, on every single job.

How we work

The principles behind
every job we do.

01

Careful preparation

The finish is only as good as the work beneath it. We take time with surfaces, fillers and primer — so the end result lasts.

02

Quality products

03

Tidy & respectful

Your home is treated with care from the moment we arrive. Floors protected, furniture covered, and everything spotless when we leave.

04

Honest pricing

Clear, itemised quotes before any work begins. No hidden costs, no surprises on the final invoice — just straightforward, fair pricing.
